Music

Mainly drums

I started playing drums when I was in middle school and I’ve never stopped. Over the years I’ve played in multiple bands and learned from some great players! I picked up guitar when I was in high school, and I’m currently working on solo singer-songwriter songs on acoustic guitar. Music’s a part of my life that will never go away and I can’t wait for any future musical endeavors!

I’m always open to new musical inquiries — if you need a drummer, reach out: oscaryarbrough1@gmail.com.

What I play, what I use

  • Drums, guitar, vocals — mainly drums though.
  • Electronic drum sounds, live and in the DAW.
  • Logic Pro X and Ableton Live.
  • I come prepared with all my own hardware.

Music Lessons

Open to everyone — in person or online, whichever works for you.

Teaching philosophy

Music is about having fun and connecting with people. I believe that everyone has music inside of them and craves to be able to say something with that voice. In my lessons I prioritize having fun, building a solid foundation, and a holistic understanding of the fundamentals. However, every student is different and I love to make adjustments depending on my student’s needs and what they want to say with their musical voice.

Rates & booking

Rates are flexible — tell me what you are after and we will work it out. The first lesson is free, so you can find out whether we get on before anyone pays anyone.

Beginners are welcome, and so are players who already have a kit and want to fix something specific. If you are not sure which you are, that is fine too.

To book a first lesson, email me: oscaryarbrough1@gmail.com.
